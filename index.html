Learning roadmap

Three levels from zero English to IELTS exam readiness.

The first release should guide students through clear milestones, short lessons, and visible progress.

Level 1

Foundation English

Alphabet, pronunciation, basic grammar, sentence building, translation practice, and mini quizzes.

  • Bangla support for every lesson
  • Visual vocabulary and audio practice
  • Short, confidence-building exercises
Level 2

Intermediate English

Grammar expansion, conversation practice, paragraph writing, reading comprehension, and vocabulary challenges.

  • More independent practice
  • Speaking confidence exercises
  • Revision plans based on weak areas
Level 3

IELTS Preparation

Listening, reading, writing, and speaking practice with mock tests, feedback, and band score guidance.

  • Exam-style timing and scoring
  • Mock interviews and test history
  • Analytics for band improvement
